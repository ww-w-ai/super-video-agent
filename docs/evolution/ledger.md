# Self-evolution ledger

What real film work taught the skill, and what was done about it.

## Procedure

No earlier ledger or evolution rule existed in this repo. The ai-native-cowork method
(`shared/references/cowork-method.md`, "retrospective") asks that a retrospective centre on self-evolution,
keep its outputs inside the repository, and promote a candidate only after repeated evidence. This ledger
applies that with the owner's rules (2026-10-16): "4가지 관점으로 평가, 보수적으로, 채택 안된건 남겨두고
다음에 또 나타나면 카운팅".

- **Rubric (owner, 2026-10-29; replaces the agent-made Q/E/S/G 0–3 lenses and the count ≥ 3 auto-promotion).**
  Owner: "이 두가지는 채점이 아니라 통과 y/n 로 두자"; "효율이라는게 애매하다 성능이 잘 나오려면 loop 를 돌리는게
  일반적인데 그러면 시간, 효율이 안나오거든 이건 그냥 빼자. 안전 규칙 준수는 필수니까 두고"; "반복적으로 나오고 있느냐 이건
  점수는 아니고 그냥 횟수로 (다른 점수가 높으면 횟수가 낮아도 채택, 다른 점수가 낮으면 횟수가 높아도 채택 안될 수 있음)";
  "좋아 이 기준으로 양쪽 스킬에 모두 적용".
  1. **Gate, y/n (required, not scored): safety and rules.** The change cannot make an agent break an owner rule or spend
     money without asking. A candidate that fails it is never adopted. Efficiency is not a gate: quality loops are expected.
  2. **Scores 0–10, the owner's four lenses** (channel-autopilot `references/policies/review-qa.md`): **L** long-term value,
     **B** generality, **S** side effects (10 = none, the safest; 0 = breaks existing films or users), **N** new-film-type need
     (N ≥ 4 justifies low generality).
  3. **Count**: the number of separate evolution checks in which the item appeared (one check counts once, however many
     films showed it; those films are the evidence). A raw count, not a score. Seen again later: count + 1 and a new "last
     seen", no new row.
  **Decide on the whole picture, conservatively.** High scores adopt even at count 1; low scores may stay unadopted at a high
  count; nothing is promoted automatically. Guide: L + B + S around 21 of 30 or more with S ≥ 7, or N ≥ 4 with high L and S.
  The evidence must be concrete (a file:line or a measured incident); an upside-only item (S = 10) with concrete evidence is
  accepted; uncertain means **pending** with its count; project preferences stay in the project. Final test: the updated
  skill still recreates the chess documentary and the LEGO build film as they are. Each candidate is shown in one block:
  content, current state, gate, L B S N, count, side effects, recommendation.
- **History.** Until 2026-10-29 rows were scored on Q outcome quality, E efficiency, S safety, G generality (0–3 each),
  adopted only when the evidence was concrete, the change small and local, G ≥ 2 and safe for existing users, and a pending
  item with count ≥ 3 was promoted automatically (owner 2026-10-16: "4가지 관점으로 평가, 보수적으로, 채택 안된건 남겨두고
  다음에 또 나타나면 카운팅"). The Q E S G columns of the table keep those old scores; the new scores of re-scored rows are
  in their reason.
- **Rejected** = not the skill's to change (environment, a project's own tool, not reproduced). Rejected rows
  stay so a recurrence is recognised.
